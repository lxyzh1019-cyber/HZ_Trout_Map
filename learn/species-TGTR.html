How to recognise it

  • A maze of wavy, worm-like lines over the whole body, like a tiger's stripes.
  • No clear round spots.
  • Lower fins often orange with a white front edge.

Where in Alberta

Alberta stocks tiger trout in some lakes and ponds. They do not occur in the wild in Alberta.

Note

A tiger trout is a cross between a brown trout and a brook trout. It cannot breed. Every tiger trout in a lake was stocked, so they disappear if stocking stops.
