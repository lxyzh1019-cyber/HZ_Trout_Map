How to recognise it

  • A tall, sail-like fin on the back, with blue, green or red spots and edges.
  • A small mouth and a small head.
  • Silver-grey to purple body, with a few dark spots near the front.

Where in Alberta

Arctic grayling are native to Alberta. They live in cool, clear streams and rivers in the north and west of the province, in the Peace and Athabasca river basins. Alberta has stocked grayling in a few lakes in past years.

Note

Grayling have declined in many Alberta rivers. Many waters are catch-and-release. Check the regulations before you keep one, and handle them gently.
