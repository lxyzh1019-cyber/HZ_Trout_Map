How to recognise it

  • Many small black spots on the back, the sides and the tail.
  • A pink or red band along each side. It can be faint on fish from a lake.
  • Silver to olive-green body. No red slash under the jaw.

Where in Alberta

Rainbow trout are the trout Alberta stocks most often. You find them in stocked lakes and ponds across the province.

A native strain, the Athabasca rainbow trout, lives in streams of the upper Athabasca River basin. It is a species at risk. Check the regulations before you fish there.

Note

Rainbow and cutthroat trout can breed together. A rainbow with a faint red mark under the jaw may be a cross.
