How to recognise it

  • Golden-brown to olive body, lighter on the belly.
  • Large black spots, and red or orange spots, often with pale rings.
  • Few or no spots on the tail.

Where in Alberta

Brown trout are not native to Alberta. They first came from Europe. Wild brown trout live in some rivers and streams in the south and the foothills. Alberta also stocks them in some lakes.

Note

Brown trout are wary. They often feed most in low light and at night. Large ones eat other fish.
