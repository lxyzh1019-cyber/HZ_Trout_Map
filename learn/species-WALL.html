How to recognise it

  • Large, glassy eyes that shine in low light.
  • Two fins on the back. The front one is spiny, with a dark blotch at its rear base.
  • A white tip on the lower part of the tail.
  • Olive-gold body with darker bands on the back. Sharp teeth.

Where in Alberta

Walleye live in many larger lakes and rivers in central and northern Alberta, and in some in the south. Alberta also stocks walleye in some lakes.

Note

Walleye feed most in low light: dawn, dusk, cloud and choppy water. Limits for walleye differ from lake to lake. Some lakes need a special harvest licence to keep one. Check the regulations for each lake.

The Bite score in this app is built for trout. Use it with care for walleye.
