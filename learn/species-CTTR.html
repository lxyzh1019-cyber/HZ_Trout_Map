How to recognise it

  • A red or orange slash under each side of the lower jaw. It gives the fish its name.
  • Black spots on the sides, more of them toward the tail.
  • No pink band along the side, or only a faint one.

Where in Alberta

Cutthroat trout live in mountain and foothill waters in the south of the province. Alberta stocks cutthroat trout in some lakes. The native kind in Alberta is the westslope cutthroat trout.

Note

Cutthroat and rainbow trout can breed together. Their young can have marks of both. Many cutthroat waters have strict limits. Check the regulations for each water.
