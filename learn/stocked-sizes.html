Alberta publishes a report of every fish it stocks. For each lake it gives the species, the number of fish, their length at stocking and the date.

The length is the size of the fish on the day it went into the lake. It is not the size you will catch.

The app never guesses growth

Fish grow at different speeds in different lakes. Growth depends on food, water temperature and how the fish get through the winter. A lake can also lose its fish to winterkill.

So the app shows only what Alberta published: the length and the date. It does not add growth to the stocked length.

Fry and catchables

  • Fry and fingerlings are small, young fish. They are stocked to grow in the lake. You will not catch them soon after stocking.
  • Catchables are 20 cm or longer at stocking. They are big enough to catch soon after they go in.

A lake stocked with catchables this spring can have fish to catch this season. A lake stocked with fry needs time before the fish are worth catching.

Check the regulations

Size limits and catch limits come from the Alberta sportfishing regulations, not from the stocking report. Check them for each lake.
