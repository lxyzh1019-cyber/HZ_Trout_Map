How to recognise it

  • A long body and a long, flat snout like a duck's bill.
  • Rows of pale, bean-shaped spots on a green body.
  • One fin on the back, set far back near the tail.
  • Many sharp teeth. Use pliers to take out the hook.

Where in Alberta

Northern pike are native to Alberta. They live in most lakes and slow rivers across the province, except high in the mountains.

Note

Pike hunt in weedy shallows, often in clear water and daylight. Limits and size rules for pike differ by water. Check the regulations for each lake.

The Bite score in this app is built for trout. Use it with care for pike.
