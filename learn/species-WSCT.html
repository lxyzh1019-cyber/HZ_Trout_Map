How to recognise it

  • A red or orange slash under the lower jaw.
  • Small black spots, mostly toward the tail and above the side line.
  • Few spots on the front, lower part of the body.
  • Often a yellow or pink tint on the sides and belly.

Where in Alberta

Westslope cutthroat trout are native to Alberta. They live in cold mountain and foothill streams and lakes, in the headwaters of the Bow and Oldman river basins.

Note

The Alberta population is listed as Threatened under the federal Species at Risk Act. Many of its waters are catch-and-release. Check the regulations before you fish, and handle these fish gently.
