How to recognise it

  • Pale, worm-like lines on a dark olive back.
  • Pale spots and small red spots with blue rings on the sides.
  • A white front edge, then a black stripe, on the lower fins.
  • A nearly square tail.

Where in Alberta

Brook trout are not native to Alberta. They come from eastern North America. Alberta stocks them in many lakes and ponds. Wild brook trout also live in many foothill streams.

Note

The brook trout is a char, like the native bull trout. In streams, brook trout compete with native bull trout and cutthroat trout. Bull trout have no blue rings around their spots and no worm-like lines. Alberta's regulations protect bull trout. Check them, and release any bull trout you catch.
