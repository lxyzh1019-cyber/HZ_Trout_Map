The barometer measures air pressure. For fishing, the direction of change matters. The number itself tells you very little.

Steady or slowly rising

This is normal weather. Fish feed on their usual pattern. Fish the usual times: early, late and under cloud.

A sharp fall before a front

Pressure drops fast as a storm front comes in. Fish often feed hard for a short time before it arrives. If you are on the water, this can be the best hour of the day.

Watch the sky as well. A front can bring strong wind and lightning. Get off the water before it reaches you.

After a front

Behind a front, pressure rises and the sky clears. High pressure and a bright sky often mean a slow bite. Fish deeper, fish slower, and try the low-light hours.

How the app uses it

The Bite score looks at the pressure trend, not the pressure value. The trend has a weight of 15 out of 100. It counts for less on days further ahead, because those forecasts are less sure.
